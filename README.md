Skybound Archipelago is an original chase-view flight game in plain Canvas 2D: you fly a little prop plane, the Kestrel, over a turquoise sea and a chain of green islands with sandy beaches and a lighthouse, seen from behind the aircraft with a lit cockpit panel — artificial horizon, altimeter and airspeed dial — and altitude, speed and heading on the HUD. Thread all eight glowing rings strung between the islands to score points and win back time and fuel, then bring her home to the runway on the largest island and land gently — slow, straight and soft — for a graded touchdown from C to S. Bank with A/D or the arrow keys, pitch with the up and down arrows, work the throttle with W/S, pause with P; three planes, one clock, and a stall that drops the nose if you let her get slow.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
